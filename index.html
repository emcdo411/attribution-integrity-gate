Chapter Two

One Gated Decision. Then The Whole Backlog.

The exercise gates one number. A Council carries dozens of items, nearly all marked priority one. The AI Council Decision Layer scores every item on five signals, caps what reaches the executive at three decisions, five risks, and five watch items, and routes any AI-touched client output through the gate. The exercise scenario sits in the sandbox as one backlog row. It scores 5, lands as Decision 1, and the gate returns HOLD.

GATE VERDICT: HOLD

Sportsbook reallocation. Model disagrees with holdout at 71%, two feeds stale, no named exception owner, suppression coverage unverified in two states. Way back: refresh, quantify, name, confirm, re-gate.
